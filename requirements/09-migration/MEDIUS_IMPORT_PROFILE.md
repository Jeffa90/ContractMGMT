# Medius Import Profile

Provide a configurable starter mapping for exported Medius contract metadata. Do not assume identical column names. The wizard must permit explicit mapping of Agreement Reference, title, description, owner, status, vendor number/name, SAP Outline Agreement Number, business unit, plant, agreement type, currency, value, category, subcategory, extension type, notice period, Start Date, Effective Date, Registered Date, Review Date and End Date.

Historical Effective contracts may be imported as locked legacy records after validation. Preserve source ID, source status and extraction timestamp.
