# Portability and No-Code Requirements

The platform must move between businesses through configuration, not source-code forks. Tenant/organisation abstraction, generic identity, storage and integration adapters, metadata-driven fields/forms, workflow/rules/approval designers, terminology, branding, localisation, feature flags, solution packs, import/export and upgrade support are mandatory.

Business administrators configure operational requirements without coding. Production infrastructure and security may still require authorised IT or a managed provider.
